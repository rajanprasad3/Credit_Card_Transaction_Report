## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **Power BI Dashboard** built to analyze credit card transaction and customer data across **52 weeks**. The goal is to uncover spending patterns, revenue trends, and customer behavior across demographics, states, card types, and expenditure categories — enabling smarter decisions for **marketing, product strategy, and customer segmentation**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Track weekly credit card revenue and transaction volume
- Segment customers by age group, income level, gender, and state
- Analyze spending patterns across card types and expenditure categories
- Identify high-value customer segments for targeted campaigns
- Support data-driven decisions for product and marketing teams

---

## 📐 Key Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Revenue** | Total revenue generated from credit card transactions |
| **Total Transaction Amount** | Sum of all transaction amounts across the period |
| **Total Transaction Count** | Number of transactions processed |
| **Total Interest Earned** | Interest revenue from outstanding balances |
| **Customer Satisfaction Score (CSS)** | Average customer rating across segments |
| **Activation Rate** | % of credit cards actively used by customers |
| **Delinquent Rate** | % of customers with overdue payments |

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 💰 Blue card holders contribute the highest share of total revenue among all card types
- 👨 Male customers account for a larger portion of total transaction volume vs female customers
- 🏙️ Texas, New York, and California are the top 3 states by transaction amount
- 🛒 Bills, Entertainment, and Fuel are the top expenditure categories
- 📅 Revenue peaks consistently in Q3 across the 52-week analysis period
- 👴 Customers aged 40-50 generate the most revenue across all age groups
- 🎓 Graduates contribute more to total revenue compared to other education levels
